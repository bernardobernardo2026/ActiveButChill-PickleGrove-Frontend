Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a court? ▾
Go to the Schedule page, pick an open time slot on your preferred court, then confirm your booking.
How do I cancel my booking? ▾
Open your booking under Bookings and select Cancel at least 24 hours before your scheduled time.
How check my booking? ▾
Your upcoming and past bookings are listed under the Bookings section of your account.
Can I rent equipment? ▾
Yes, paddles and balls can be added during checkout on the booking summary page.
