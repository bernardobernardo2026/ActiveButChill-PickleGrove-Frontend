Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a court? ▾
Go to the Schedule page, pick an open time slot on your preferred court, then confirm your booking.
How do I cancel my booking? ▾
Open My Bookings from your Profile page and select Cancel Booking at least 24 hours before your scheduled time.
How do I check my booking? ▾
Open My Bookings from your Profile page to see your Today, Upcoming, and History bookings.
Can I rent equipment? ▾
Yes, paddles and balls can be added during checkout on the booking summary page.
