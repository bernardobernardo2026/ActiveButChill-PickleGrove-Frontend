Tutorial: Where Can I know The Court Rules and Info

Open Court Rules and Info from the landing page or from the Booking Details page. It lists the operating hours, court rates and court rules, including the 24-hour cancellation policy.

Tutorial: How to know when a time and date is available?

Go to Schedule and pick a date. Only open times are listed, with the rate for each hour. Dates marked red are fully booked, and striped dates are closed by the court (for example, for an event or maintenance).

Tutorial: How do I book both courts?

Pick your hours on Court 1, then switch to Court 2 and pick your hours there. Your Court 1 picks stay saved, and each court button shows how many hours you picked. "Your selection" lists all your times: the same hours on both courts appear as "Courts 1 & 2". Hours on each court must be back-to-back.

Tutorial: How I Can Know My Transactions Is Truly Confirmed?

After you confirm, a Digital Receipt shows "Booking Confirmed!" with your court, date, time, payment and reference number. You can also see your booking anytime under My Bookings.

Tutorial: How do I cancel a booking?

Click My Bookings in the header. Under Today or Upcoming, select Cancel Booking and confirm. Cancellations must be made at least 24 hours before your booking; later cancellations are non-refundable.
