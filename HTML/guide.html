Tutorial: Where Can I know The Court Rules and Info

By clicking a selected time and date you can view the court rules and info and see the listed rules and information stating specific rules that must be followed and helpful information about the selected court.

Tutorial: How to know when a time and date is available?

See in the Schedule Chart if the certain time and date is available. If it's blocked with a red mark, it's unavailable — otherwise pick another time and date to book.

Tutorial: How I Can Know My Transactions Is Truly Confirmed?

To know when a transaction is confirmed, look at the top left of the screen. You will see two different messages: one describes that the payment is received and shows your digital receipt as proof of transaction; the other shows the payment was rejected, meaning you'll need to attempt again. If the issue persists, contact our support team.

Tutorial: How to Delete My Account and Unsubscribe to memberships

You can unsubscribe by going to My Account and clicking Subscriptions, where you can see the details of when it will end. Take note to cancel any membership or subscription before the said date of the next month to avoid further charges to your e-wallet.
